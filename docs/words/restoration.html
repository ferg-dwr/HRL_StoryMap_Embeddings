Restoration (say it: res-tuh-RAY-shun) — noun

Restoration means helping a damaged place work the way it used to. It isn't about making a place look pretty. It's about getting the place doing its old jobs again — holding water, growing food, giving fish somewhere to grow up. To restore (say it: ree-STOR) a floodplain, you give the river back some of the room it used to have.
