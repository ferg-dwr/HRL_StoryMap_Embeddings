Ecology (say it: ee-KAH-luh-jee) — noun

Ecology is the study of how living things — like animals, plants, and people — interact with each other and the world around them. It's kind of like learning the rules of a really big neighborhood: who lives where, what they eat, how they help each other, and what happens when something changes. Scientists who study ecology are called ecologists (say it: ee-KAH-luh-jists).
